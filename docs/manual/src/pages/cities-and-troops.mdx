# Cities and troops

Cities are your economy; troops are everything else. This page explains how cities produce, how stacks grow and cap out, and how cities change hands.

## Cities produce every tick

The game advances in **ticks** — four per second. Each city you own produces **1 troop per tick**, automatically, with no orders needed. Four ticks per second means an untouched city grows at a steady 4 troops per second until it saturates.

## Saturation: the cap of 30

A city cell holds at most **30 troops**. Once it reaches 30, production stops for that tick — no overflow, no waste beyond the cap. A saturated city resumes producing the moment troops march away and free up room.

Open (non-city) land cells hold at most **30 troops** as well, so a big stack arriving in a small cell simply fills it to the cap.

## Troops are your currency

Troops are not just fighters:

- They garrison cells and capture cities.
- They are delivered through pipes to wherever you need them — flow adds troops at the destination without draining the source (see [Pipes](./pipes)).
- Special weapons consume them: a paratroop order spends troops from the source cell, and so does every gun shot.
- Unfed troops decay away (see [Pipes](./pipes)) — troops you cannot supply are troops you will lose.

## Capturing cities

Cities change hands through occupation, not destruction:

- When troops of one player occupy a city cell owned by another player, the city **transfers immediately** to the occupying force.
- The troops doing the capturing stay put — a captured city keeps its troop count, now serving its new owner.
- A city with defenders fights first: incoming troops must win the engagement in that cell before they count as occupants (see [Combat](./combat)).

Because elimination requires losing all troops AND all cities (see [Objective](./objective)), your cities are your lifeline: even a single recaptured city puts you back in production.

## The opening loop

A typical opening: let your starting cities build up, lay pipes toward the middle of the board, and stream fresh troops forward while keeping your home cells topped up. Production is free — forgetting to spend it is the only waste.
