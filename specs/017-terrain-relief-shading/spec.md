## Problem Statement

A player cannot read how troop flow will go from the terrain itself. Tiles that look identical can have very different elevations, nothing on a tile says anything about its neighbours, and the only slope signal — the pipe-slope triangles — appears after a pipe has been laid, so it cannot be used to *plan* a route. The biome hue bands answer "how high is this cell?" in four hue families, but relative direction and steepness are unrepresented. This feature adds the missing second channel: each land tile's edges are shaded by the sign and size of the elevation delta with the neighbouring tile, so ridges, drops, and gentle slopes are visible on the board itself — before any pipe exists — while the absolute-elevation hue channel and every mechanical behaviour stay exactly as they are.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Read the terrain's slope from the board (Priority: P1)

As a player, I want each land tile's edges shaded by whether its neighbours are higher or lower, with stronger shading for bigger differences, so that I can see ridges and downhill routes and plan a pipe network before I commit a pipe.

**Why this priority**: This is the whole feature; without it nothing else here matters.

**Independent Test**: Render scripted `MapView` fixtures in the browser pixel-readback harness and assert: a tile with a higher visible land neighbour on one side and a lower one on the opposite side paints opposite relief on those two edges (one band lighter than its relief-free control, the other darker); the measured magnitude grows monotonically across a `|Δelevation|` sweep from 1 to 255 and reaches the same fraction of each zone's maximum in every zone; and all four zones, including the brightest (Peaks), clear FR-006. The ≥3:1 band-to-band figure and the floor are measured on FR-006's **per-zone** reference fixture — that zone's largest balanced delta pair, never a pair copied from another zone (zone 1's is `|Δ| = 127` and `128`; Peaks's is `46` and `209`) — while the 1…255 sweep asserts monotonicity and proportionality alone and makes no band-to-band claim.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** two adjacent visible land tiles of unequal elevation, **When** the board paints, **Then** each carries relief on the shared edge whose luminance direction encodes the delta sign.
2. **Given** a tile with a higher neighbour on one side and a lower one on the opposite side, **When** it paints, **Then** both edges carry relief in opposite luminance directions, and the two bands measure ≥3:1 apart on that zone's FR-006 reference fixture (that zone's largest balanced delta pair, not an arbitrary delta).
3. **Given** a visible land tile whose neighbours are all non-land (fog-hidden, water, off-board), **When** it paints, **Then** all four edges are neutral and its pixels match a relief-free control paint of the same tile.
4. **Given** the same delta magnitude in two different biome zones, **When** both paint, **Then** the magnitude follows one continuous function of `|Δelevation|` — the same fraction of each zone's maximum treatment, not a per-zone bucket or band table.
5. **Given** the brightest zone (Peaks: `hue: 200`, `saturationPct: 15`, lightness 80–95), **When** a Peaks tile paints FR-006's Peaks reference fixture (own elevation 209 against neighbours 255 and 0, so `|Δ| = 46` and `209` — that zone's largest balanced pair, never zone 1's `127`/`128`), **Then** the two bands are ≥3:1 apart and each clears FR-006's floor against the tile's relief-free control, for a higher and for a lower neighbour.

### User Story 2 - Relief never reveals what the fog hides (Priority: P1)

As a player, I want relief to appear only where I can already see both cells, so that fog stays opaque and I cannot learn a hidden cell's elevation from the edge of my horizon.

**Why this priority**: A leak here would hand players (and any future client) hidden map data — a correctness and fairness defect, not a cosmetic one.

**Independent Test**: Render fixtures whose neighbour slots hold water, an absent (fog-hidden or beyond-horizon) cell, and an off-board position; assert the edge pixels are identical to the same fixture painted with no relief anywhere, and that changing the *hidden* neighbour's elevation between two otherwise identical fixtures produces byte-identical pixels.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a land tile at the edge of the visibility horizon, **When** it paints, **Then** the fog-facing edge is neutral and no relief, outline, or synthesized shading appears on it.
2. **Given** a land tile with water on one side and visible land on another, **When** it paints, **Then** the water side is neutral and the land side carries relief only if the delta is non-zero.
3. **Given** the same fixture twice, with the hidden neighbour's elevation differing between the runs, **When** both paint, **Then** the pixels are identical.

### User Story 3 - Learn the two channels from the manual and the in-game legend (Priority: P2)

As a player, I want the manual and the in-game help legend to explain absolute biome elevation (hue) separately from relative edge relief (luminance), in words that match what the board actually draws, so that I can use the new signal instead of guessing at it.

**Why this priority**: A relief channel nobody can decode adds noise, and the manual's elevation sentence is already false (`docs/manual/src/pages/the-board.mdx:26`) — an existing documentation bug this change set carries (AGENTS.md workflow rule 4; constitution Principle IV), not a spec 007 FR-012 obligation.

**Independent Test**: Read the updated manual page and the help overlay's Symbol Legend; confirm each carries one entry/concept for absolute elevation and one for relative relief, and that the stale "dark = low, light = high" / "dark green … bright green" claims are gone.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the updated `docs/manual/src/pages/the-board.mdx`, **When** a reader reaches the elevation-shading section, **Then** it explains that hue encodes how high a cell is and that edge shading encodes whether a neighbour is higher or lower, and it no longer claims green-to-green shading.
2. **Given** the in-match help overlay's Symbol Legend, **When** it renders, **Then** it contains distinct entries for biome-elevation hue and for edge relief, and no entry claims "dark = low, light = high".

### Edge Cases

- **No visible land neighbours** (all four sides water/fog/off-board): a flat biome field — no relief at all.
- **Both delta signs on one tile** (higher on one side, lower on another): both treatments, in opposite luminance directions.
- **Adjacent tiles facing each other**: each tile's bands come from its own deltas alone, so the two facing bands carry opposite directions by construction (the shared delta flips sign) — no ridge/valley pairing state, no cross-tile coupling, no paint-order dependence.
- **Board edge and corners**: a missing neighbour is neutral at any board size; a corner tile has two such edges, an edge tile one.
- **Fog or water on one side only, and a delta of zero**: that direction is neutral — no relief crosses, hugs, or hints at the fog boundary, and a zero delta (common on smoothed terrain) is a valid, required "no step" reading.
- **Maximum delta** (`|Δ|` up to 255, e.g. 0 next to 255): admissible, and relief MUST NOT paint outside the tile's own rect (AC-003).
- **Zoom extremes** (cell size 32–96 CSS px, 005 FR-017): FR-006's criterion holds at the 32 px minimum, and relief stays confined at the 96 px maximum.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The console MUST shade land-tile edges by the *relative* elevation against each adjacent cell that is itself a visible land tile: for each of the four edge directions, the rendered relief MUST be a function of the sign and magnitude of `neighbourElevation − ownElevation` alone. Relief is rendered only on edges shared by two visible land tiles; every other edge is neutral (FR-004). It is computed from the fog-filtered view snapshot alone, with no role input, so a spectator's board computes relief exactly as a player's does. The tile-uniform treatment is replaced (FR-005), and relief stays inside the tile's own rect at every zoom step.
- **FR-002**: Relief luminance MUST encode the *sign* of the delta: on one tile, an edge toward a higher visible land neighbour and an edge toward a lower one MUST render as mutually distinguishable luminance directions (FR-006a) — one the highlight, the other the shadow — so uphill and downhill neighbours are distinguishable without a pipe. The sign → direction assignment MUST be a single fixed renderer constant, the same for every tile and zone, and documented for players (FR-011). Where one direction cannot meet FR-006b's floor against a zone's background, relief MAY adapt its own base luminance by a **documented per-zone constant** — one number for the zone; the adaptation MUST NOT alter the magnitude mapping (FR-003), vary per tile, or become a per-zone contrast threshold or outcome table.
- **FR-003**: Relief magnitude MUST be continuous and proportional to the raw elevation delta: for a fixed edge it MUST increase with `|Δelevation|`, MUST NOT be quantized into bands or routed through a per-zone bucket table, and MUST be the same function in every biome zone. Magnitude is measured as the band's contrast against that zone's relief-free control (FR-006b); FR-002's zone-constant adaptation MUST leave the mapping's shape intact, so the fraction of a zone's maximum treatment reached at a given `|Δ|` is the same in all four zones. `Δelevation = 0` MUST produce no relief.
- **FR-004**: An edge MUST be neutral — no bevel of either sign and no synthesized, inferred, or borrowed neighbour elevation — when the neighbouring cell is (a) water, (b) absent from the fog-filtered view (outside the visibility horizon or otherwise hidden), or (c) outside the board bounds. On such an edge the painted pixels MUST equal those of the same tile painted with that edge carrying no relief, so a hidden elevation can never be revealed or implied (constitution Principle II — the client renders only what the server sent; spec 002's zero-leakage invariant SC-001; the structural rule that cells outside the horizon are absent from `MapView.cells`).
- **FR-005**: The uniform per-tile inner shadow MUST no longer be rendered; delta-based edge relief (FR-001) replaces it. This resolves the conflict with 005's absorbed `024-FR-013` ("The inner shadow on land tiles MUST remain unchanged"), rewritten by amendment A-01. The per-tile biome gradient (`024-FR-011`) and the zone 2–3 contour hints (`024-FR-012`) MUST remain unchanged, as MUST the DOM overlay's transparency (`024-FR-020`).
- **FR-006**: Relief MUST be distinguishable on all four biome zones, including the brightest (Peaks: `hue: 200`, `saturationPct: 15`, `lightnessMin: 80`, `lightnessMax: 95`). Its two measurable criteria are asserted **per zone** on that zone's **reference fixture** — the largest *balanced* delta pair the zone can carry: own elevation nearest 127.5 within the zone against neighbours 0 and 255, with `|Δ|` stated as to the lower / to the higher neighbour — zone 0 → own 80, `80`/`175`; zone 1 → own 128, `128`/`127`; zone 2 → own 161, `161`/`94`; zone 3 → own 209, `209`/`46` (own elevation is what selects the zone, so each zone needs its own fixture). Only zone 1's fixture is balanced; the other three sit at a zone edge, so one direction necessarily carries the smaller delta shown, and on zones 0–2 the floor is binding on that band — but at Peaks it binds on the larger `209` band, because a Peaks band toward the lower neighbour is bounded by white against the light gradient end (Clarifications, v1.5). **(a)** the two signed relief bands on the fixture MUST be mutually distinguishable — measured WCAG relative-luminance contrast ≥ 3:1 between the bands; **(b)** each band MUST differ from the same pixel in an identical relief-free control render of that fixture by at least **1.2:1** — one documented floor, a single constant for all four zones, never a per-zone threshold table (that would reintroduce the bucket shape FR-003 forbids). Both criteria are on the rendered result, not on any particular colour, alpha, or blend mode (those are the plan's choice), and are scoped to that zone's reference `|Δ|`: they MUST NOT be restated as holding at every `|Δ|` — at `|Δ| = 1` both bands sit ≈1.00:1 from the control, so a band-to-band ≥3:1 claim there is unreachable, and flooring the mapping to force it would break FR-003's continuity and proportionality. The 1…255 `|Δ|` sweep fixture asserts **only** monotonicity and proportionality (FR-003, AC-002) and makes no band-to-band or floor claim. Measurements MUST sample away from the sub-pass 1e contour diagonals (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.10)` every 6 px, zones 2–3), whose composite can move a sampled Peaks pixel from 1.91:1 to 2.36:1 for the light direction. Two consequences forced by that arithmetic are recorded in Clarifications: the *light* direction on Peaks is luminance-capped at ≈1.31:1, so a per-band "≥3:1 against the tile's own background" is unreachable by any implementation and MUST NOT be restated; and Peaks's unbalanced `46`/`209` pair — never zone 1's balanced `127`/`128` — sets the Peaks floor and band-to-band figures, so neither figure may be inherited from zone 1. A zone whose delta-bearing edges fail either criterion fails.
- **FR-007**: Relief MUST be purely visual. Flow rates, the uphill stall threshold, `classifyPipeSlope`/`pipeIntensity`, the pipe slope colours, and the slope triangles stay exactly as specified by 005 FR-013 and absorbed `024-FR-014`. Relief MUST NOT alter pipe rendering, order preflight, or any wire message.
- **FR-008**: Relief MUST be produced by the console's Canvas 2D painter. A WebGL/WebGL2 or 3D renderer for this effect is explicitly out of scope; the per-tile DOM invariant is `021-FR-004`'s (asserted by AC-007), so it is not restated here.
- **FR-009**: Every colour relief paints MUST come from `@europa/design` tokens (012 FR-009; absorbed `021-FR-006`/`021-FR-011`): `packages/console/src` MUST NOT gain a hex or rgb literal for relief (guard G-04, the running `check:no-literals` scan). Any new token MUST be additive and MUST land in `packages/design/src/tokens.ts` and `DESIGN.md` § 1.1 in the same commit (012 FR-017/FR-018; a minor change per `DESIGN.md` § 6). Because the relief pairing is delta-dependent and canvas-painted, it is documented as a note in the `DESIGN.md` N-3/N-4 family and MUST NOT become a § 3 measured row — § 3's own preamble defines every row as a measured pairing pinned by a computed-style check over a rendered CSS fixture, which cannot read canvas pixels or a delta; and G-07, the guard behind that claim, is deferred per 012's Scope Note, so no runner would read it anyway. A documented pairing that cannot be asserted mechanically MUST have its provenance and guard gap recorded in that note.
- **FR-010**: Relief MUST be a pure, deterministic function of visible view data only — own terrain and elevation, the neighbour's terrain and elevation, and whether the neighbour is present in the fog-filtered view. It MUST NOT read wall-clock time, use unseeded randomness, depend on paint order or accumulated state, or require a new serialized view field. Painting the same `MapView` twice MUST produce byte-identical pixels, and so MUST painting the same cells after inserting them into `MapView.cells` in a different order (`MapView.cells` is a `Map`, so iteration order is insertion order — an order-sensitive treatment would ship a board that depends on how the view was built). The 1000-tick golden suite (`packages/console/tests/integration/determinism.test.ts`, 005 SC-002) MUST stay green with its hash unchanged.
- **FR-011**: The player-facing description MUST ship in the same change set: (a) `docs/manual/src/pages/the-board.mdx` MUST explain relief and MUST correct its stale elevation sentence — "from dark green at the lowest ground to bright green at the peaks" describes no shipped biome zone (hues are 210/170/30/200); (b) the in-match help overlay's Symbol Legend (absorbed `018-FR-004`, `packages/console/src/ui/help-overlay.tsx`) MUST gain entries distinguishing absolute biome elevation (hue) from relative edge relief (luminance) and MUST NOT keep "dark = low, light = high" — also false under the shipped zones, where zone 1 (Fractured Ice, 26–40) is darker than zone 0 (Ice Plains, 38–52) while being higher. The manual edit is required because `the-board.mdx:26` is already false, not because 007 FR-012 triggers on this feature (it is not gameplay behaviour). The same change set corrects `DESIGN.md` and the `palette.ts` comment through amendments A-03 and A-05, every corrected contrast figure produced by a test that reads `TOKENS.color.biomeZones`, never a hand-copied literal.

### Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-001 (Performance)**: The paint budget stays green with relief enabled, as one merge gate: `test:perf` Q-P01 (`packages/console/tests/integration/perf.test.ts`) keeps its min-of-round-medians 8 ms budget on its 32×32 fixture with only the documented `EUROPA_PERF_BUDGET_FACTOR` CI slack, **and** the suite gains a relief-enabled 48×48 fixture (2,304 cells — the supported maximum, `docs/manual/src/pages/the-board.mdx:17–20`) under that same budget, with non-zero deltas across all four zones, because four neighbour lookups per tile on the largest board is the feature's actual new cost.
- **NFR-002 (Simplicity and self-hosting)**: Zero new runtime dependencies; relief is computed in-process from the view the client already receives. No engine, protocol, or configuration surface changes (constitution Principles V and VII).

### Key Entities

None. Relief adds no data entity, no contract field, and no persisted state; it is a paint-time function of existing `MapView`/`CellRenderInfo` fields (`terrain`, `elevation`, cell presence) and `MapView.width`/`height`.

## Out of Scope

- **Any simulation or engine change, including re-tuning the pipe slope colours or their dark outline** — flow rates, the uphill stall threshold, pipe-slope classification and intensity, slope colours, and slope triangles stay exactly as they are (locked decision 1; 005 FR-013, `024-FR-014`). Where A-03's corrected figures land below a recorded target, this change set corrects the numbers and their justification only; the corrected row MUST carry either a concrete issue reference or an explicit *accepted-with-no-planned-action* annotation in the style of `DESIGN.md` N-2 — no promise with no owner. The issue number is filled in during Phase 6 and MUST NOT be invented here.
- **Removing or re-colouring the biome zones** — the four hue bands and their spec 012 tokens stay; relief is layered on top (locked decision 2).
- **A WebGL/WebGL2/3D renderer** for this effect (locked decision 7).
- **Relief in the minimap** — locked decision 6, and a plain exclusion rather than a requirement: the minimap keeps its biome colours (`024-FR-040`) through its own painter (`packages/console/src/qol/minimap.tsx`, which never calls `MapCanvas`), so a relief assertion about it could not fail.
- **A non-visual (screen-reader) elevation or relief readout** — a visual-only feature; the console's cell accessible name does not carry elevation today and is unchanged, so no accessibility regression, and an accessible readout remains a separate future feature.
- **`EuropaElevationSwatch` (`024-FR-041`)** — unchanged; no relief preview is added to it.
- **Terrain generation, smoothing, or board sizes** — no change; the disabled 64×64 size stays disabled.
- **Any edit under `europa-source/`** (SOS licence; reference only — never modified, never copied).

## Clarifications

### v1.0 (2026-10-15) — issue #182 open questions resolved; no unresolved clarification markers remain

- **Spec directory and citation**: `specs/017-terrain-relief-shading/` (the issue proposed `016-...`, occupied by the security-sweep manifest). Feature 017 was not free — spec 013 owns the `017-FR-###` block — hence full-path citations.
- **Water-tile edges and non-land neighbours** (open questions 1–2): **neutral** — relief exists only between two visible land tiles (FR-001/FR-004, locked decision 4), with no synthesized neighbour elevation and no edge hint of any kind on the fog side, because a leak at the horizon is a correctness and fairness defect, not a visual one.
- **Fate of sub-passes 1c and 1d** (open question 3): 1c (biome gradient) **kept as-is**; 1d (uniform inner shadow) **replaced** by delta-based bevels (FR-005, locked decision 5); 1e (zone 2–3 contour hints, `024-FR-012`) untouched — and the reason FR-006 pins a sampling rule.
- **Minimap, perf budget, reference screenshot** (open questions 4–6): the minimap is **unchanged**, a locked decision and an exclusion rather than a requirement (v1.0's "MUST NOT render relief" could not fail); Q-P01's 8 ms budget stays in force on its 32×32 fixture and gains a relief-enabled 48×48 fixture under the same budget (NFR-001), with nothing deferred to Phase 4; the original's `terrain.gif` is inspiration only, never an acceptance criterion (AGENTS.md decision 1; `europa-source/` never copied).
- **Light-source model**: this spec fixes the observable a test can pin — the two delta signs produce opposite, mutually distinguishable luminance directions from one fixed constant (FR-002) — and leaves the treatment (colour, alpha, band geometry) to the plan. *Which* direction is the highlight is **not** free: FR-006's arithmetic forces it (v1.5).
- **Contrast criterion and the Peaks risk**: a Peaks tile is painted `hsl(200 15% 88%)` → `hsl(200 15% 73%)` (zone midpoint 88 via `landBandColor`, `adjustBrightness(−15)` → 73), relative luminance ≈0.752 → ≈0.4987; pure white measures only ≈1.31:1 against the light end and ≈1.91:1 against the dark end, so v1.0's "≥3:1 against the tile's own background" was unreachable. Hence band-to-band ≥3:1 plus a single 1.2:1 floor against the relief-free control — one constant, never a per-zone table; the floor is weak on purpose (it fails a band that is invisible on the tile) because band-to-band must carry the feature. Both criteria are asserted per zone on that zone's reference fixture (the largest balanced delta pair the zone can carry; own elevation 80 / 128 / 161 / 209 for zones 0–3, `|Δ|` to the lower / to the higher neighbour 80/175, 128/127, 161/94, 209/46 as listed in FR-006), not universally: at `|Δ| = 1` both bands sit ≈1.00:1 from the control, so a universal band-to-band ≥3:1 is unreachable and flooring the mapping to fake it would break FR-003. The 1…255 sweep carries monotonicity/proportionality only. Only zone 1's fixture is balanced; at Peaks the pair is unbalanced (`|Δ| = 46` and `209`), so the Peaks band-to-band figure is measured between a 46 band and a 209 band — both are Peaks arithmetic and neither may be inherited from zone 1's 127/128, while the ≈1.31:1 ceiling on the light direction still caps that direction; and that same cap is why, at Peaks alone, the floor binds on the `209` band rather than the `46` band (v1.5 correction).
- **Relief-free control**: one definition, so tests cannot invent two — a relief-disabled paint of the same fixture through a documented paint option (a `PaintOptions` toggle, not a `MapView` field, so FR-010's "no new serialized view field" holds). A neighbour-substituted fixture is not the control.
- **Determinism**: relief is a paint-time function of the existing fog-filtered view with no serialized field, so the golden hash is expected to stay unchanged (FR-010); a plan that cannot do it without new view data brings back a contract change and a leak-risk review.
- **Accessibility**: relief adds a visual channel, removes none, and is not the sole encoding of anything a player needs (hue gives absolute elevation, pipe triangles give flow direction). v1.0's accessibility NFR and "no documented § 3 pairing regresses" criterion were cut at Gate 1: axe measures DOM and G-07 (itself deferred per 012's Scope Note) measures CSS, so neither reads canvas pixels against a § 3 pairing.
- **Observed stale docs carried into the change set**: `DESIGN.md` and `packages/console/src/render/palette.ts` carry terrain-shading figures computed against the retired zone values; A-03/A-05 correct them, each figure produced from `TOKENS.color.biomeZones` by a test, so a token change fails a test instead of rotting the docs (012 FR-018; constitution Principle IV).
- **Zero unresolved clarification markers remain**: every question the issue raised is answered above or carried by a requirement.

### v1.5 (2026-10-16) — the sign constant is forced; AC-003/AC-005 text corrected

- **The sign constant is forced, not chosen**: FR-002 requires one fixed sign → direction constant, but FR-006(a) and (b) together with AC-002's zone invariance determine *which* one, so FR-002's "single fixed renderer constant" is a determined value rather than a free pick. A band toward a lower Peaks neighbour is bounded by white against the tile's light gradient end (cap ≈1.31:1) and, with magnitude proportional to `|Δ|` (FR-003), a highlight at the Peaks reference `|Δ| = 46` reaches at most ≈1.06:1 — below FR-006b's 1.2:1 floor. Therefore **a higher neighbour MUST render as the darker band** for any implementation satisfying those criteria, and flipping the polarity later would fail AC-005 for Peaks. That is why the two statements above — the *Light-source model* bullet and the "floor binds on the `46` band" clause — are corrected in v1.5. No criterion changed.
