## Problem Statement

A player cannot read how troop flow will go from the terrain itself. Tiles that look identical can have very different elevations, nothing on a tile says anything about its neighbours, and the only slope signal — the pipe-slope triangles — appears after a pipe has been laid, so it cannot be used to *plan* a route. The biome hue bands answer "how high is this cell?" in four hue families, but relative height is unrepresented. This feature adds the missing second channel: each land tile's edge carries a bevel wherever that tile is strictly higher than the visible land neighbour on that side, so ledges, terraces, and drop directions are visible on the board itself — before any pipe exists — while the absolute-elevation hue channel and every mechanical behaviour stay exactly as they are. The tile face carries a fixed decorative gradient that encodes nothing; all depth comes from the bevels.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Read drops and terraces from the board (Priority: P1)

As a player, I want each land tile's edges to show where it drops to a lower neighbour, with a wider bevel for a bigger drop and a lit or shaded face depending on its direction, so that I can see ledges and downhill routes and plan a pipe network before I commit a pipe.

**Why this priority**: This is the whole feature; without it nothing else here matters.

**Independent Test**: Render scripted `MapView` fixtures in the browser pixel-readback harness and assert: a tile strictly higher than a visible land neighbour paints a bevel only on that side, never on its other sides; width is the 4 / 7 / 10 % ladder for ladder steps 1 / 2 / 3 (Δ 1–4 / 5–9 / 10+), with step 3 capped at 10 %; tone depends on the side alone — north and west lit, south and east shaded, as a lightness shift that keeps the face's hue and saturation — so a tile higher than four equal neighbours carries lighter N/W bevels and darker S/E bevels; equal heights, water, fog-absent and off-board neighbours paint nothing; and all four zones, including the brightest (Peaks), show both tones ≥ 3:1 apart and each ≥ 1.2:1 against the face gradient's worst-case stop (a lit bevel against its lightest stop, a shaded one against its darkest).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** two adjacent visible land tiles of unequal elevation, **When** the board paints, **Then** only the higher tile carries a bevel on the shared edge: Δ > 0 paints, Δ ≤ 0 paints nothing.
2. **Given** a tile strictly higher than all four visible land neighbours at the same Δ, **When** it paints, **Then** its N and W bevels are the lit tone and its S and E bevels the shaded tone, and the two tones measure ≥ 3:1 apart on each zone's FR-006 reference fixture.
3. **Given** a visible land tile whose neighbours are all non-land (fog-hidden, water, off-board) or equal in elevation, **When** it paints, **Then** all four edges are neutral and its pixels match a relief-free control paint of the same tile.
4. **Given** drops of 1, 5 and 10 in the same biome zone, **When** they paint, **Then** the bevel width follows the 4 / 7 / 10 % ladder and stops at 10 % — a step-3 drop and Δ255 paint identical bevels — while the tone ladder still moves one step further from the face for each step and saturates there.
5. **Given** the brightest zone (Peaks: `hue: 200`, `saturationPct: 15`, lightness 80–95), **When** a Peaks tile paints FR-006's Peaks reference fixture (own elevation at the zone ceiling, its visible land neighbours one lower), **Then** the lit and shaded bevels are ≥ 3:1 apart and each clears FR-006's floor against the face gradient's worst-case stop.

### User Story 2 - Relief never reveals what the fog hides (Priority: P1)

As a player, I want relief to appear only where I can already see both cells, so that fog stays opaque and I cannot learn a hidden cell's elevation from the edge of my horizon.

**Why this priority**: A leak here would hand players (and any future client) hidden map data — a correctness and fairness defect, not a cosmetic one.

**Independent Test**: Render fixtures whose neighbour slots hold water, an absent (fog-hidden or beyond-horizon) cell, and an off-board position; assert the edge pixels are identical to the same fixture painted with no relief anywhere, and that changing the *hidden* neighbour's elevation between two otherwise identical fixtures produces byte-identical pixels.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a land tile at the edge of the visibility horizon, **When** it paints, **Then** the fog-facing edge is neutral and no relief, outline, or synthesized shading appears on it.
2. **Given** a land tile with water on one side and visible land on another, **When** it paints, **Then** the water side is neutral and the land side carries relief only if the delta is non-zero.
3. **Given** the same fixture twice, with the hidden neighbour's elevation differing between the runs, **When** both paint, **Then** the pixels are identical.

### User Story 3 - Learn the two channels from the manual and the in-game legend (Priority: P2)

As a player, I want the manual and the in-game help legend to explain absolute biome elevation (hue) separately from relative edge relief (luminance), in words that match what the board actually draws, so that I can use the new signal instead of guessing at it.

**Why this priority**: A relief channel nobody can decode adds noise, and the manual's elevation sentence is already false (`docs/manual/src/pages/the-board.mdx:26`) — an existing documentation bug this change set carries (AGENTS.md workflow rule 4; constitution Principle IV), not a spec 007 FR-012 obligation.

**Independent Test**: Read the updated manual page and the help overlay's Symbol Legend; confirm each carries one entry/concept for absolute elevation and one for relative relief, and that the stale "dark = low, light = high" / "dark green … bright green" claims are gone.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the updated `docs/manual/src/pages/the-board.mdx`, **When** a reader reaches the elevation-shading section, **Then** it explains that hue encodes how high a cell is and that edge shading encodes whether a neighbour is higher or lower, and it no longer claims green-to-green shading.
2. **Given** the in-match help overlay's Symbol Legend, **When** it renders, **Then** it contains distinct entries for biome-elevation hue and for edge relief, and no entry claims "dark = low, light = high".

### Edge Cases

- **No visible land neighbours** (all four sides water/fog/off-board): a plain biome field — the face texture and no bevel at all.
- **Equal heights**: `Δ = 0` paints nothing, so two tiles at the same elevation join seamlessly.
- **A lower neighbour on one side, a higher one on another**: only the lower side carries a bevel; a higher neighbour is never painted by this tile, and the neighbour paints nothing on the shared edge either (it is not higher there).
- **Adjacent tiles facing each other**: only the higher tile's bevel exists, so a ledge has one bevel, not a ridge/valley pair — no cross-tile state and no paint-order dependence.
- **Board edge and corners**: a missing neighbour is neutral at any board size; a corner tile has two such edges, an edge tile one.
- **Fog or water on one side only**: that direction is neutral — no bevel crosses, hugs, or hints at the fog boundary.
- **Maximum delta** (`Δ` up to 255, e.g. 255 next to 0): admissible; width and tone saturate at ladder step 3, and relief MUST NOT paint outside the tile's own rect (AC-003).
- **Zoom extremes** (cell size 32–96 CSS px, 005 FR-017): the width ladder is a fraction of the cell, so FR-006's criteria hold at the 32 px minimum, and relief stays confined at the 96 px maximum.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: A land tile MUST carry a bevel on an edge **only where that tile is strictly higher than the neighbour on that side** — `Δ = ownElevation − neighbourElevation > 0` — with the neighbour itself a visible land cell. The four bevels are mitred trapezoids inside the tile's own rect: N `(0,0) (S,0) (S−w,w) (w,w)`, S `(0,S) (S,S) (S−w,S−w) (w,S−w)`, W `(0,0) (w,w) (w,S−w) (0,S)`, E `(S,0) (S,S) (S−w,S−w) (S−w,w)`, where `S` is the cell size and `w` the bevel width (FR-003). The taper marks where a ledge run ends. It is computed from the fog-filtered view snapshot alone, with no role input, so a spectator's board computes relief exactly as a player's does. Every other edge is neutral (FR-004).
- **FR-002**: One light stands at the map's NW corner. A bevel whose outward face points **north or west is lit**; one pointing **south or east is shaded**. Tone therefore depends on the side alone — never on the sign of a delta (there is no bevel for a negative one), never on which tile is higher, and never per zone or per tile beyond the zone's own face. Tone moves the face's **lightness only**, keeping its hue and saturation (FR-003). The light assignment MUST be a single fixed renderer constant, the same for every tile and zone, and documented for players (FR-011). Bevels are opaque and never leave the tile that paints them.
- **FR-003**: Width and tone step with the positive drop and saturate. **Width is 4 % / 7 % / 10 % of the tile size for ladder steps 1 / 2 / 3**, capped at 10 %. The steps are cut from the measured adjacent-land drop distribution: **step 1 covers Δ 1–4, step 2 Δ 5–9, step 3 Δ ≥ 10**; the thresholds are named renderer constants and `plan.md` D5 records the measurement they came from. **Tone moves the face's lightness and nothing else**: a lit (N/W) bevel keeps the face's hue and saturation and raises its lightness 74 % / 79 % / 84 % of the way to the lit token's lightness; a shaded (S/E) bevel lowers its lightness 40 % / 50 % / 60 % of the way to the shaded token's lightness. The ladder is deliberately stepped, not continuous — the same three steps and the same shares in every biome zone, with no per-zone table. `Δ ≤ 0` produces no bevel.
- **FR-004**: An edge MUST be neutral — no bevel and no synthesized, inferred, or borrowed neighbour elevation — when the neighbouring cell is (a) water, (b) absent from the fog-filtered view (outside the visibility horizon or otherwise hidden), (c) outside the board bounds, or (d) at the same or a higher elevation. On such an edge the painted pixels MUST equal those of the same tile painted with no relief, so a hidden elevation can never be revealed or implied, and equal heights join seamlessly (constitution Principle II — the client renders only what the server sent; spec 002's zero-leakage invariant SC-001; the structural rule that cells outside the horizon are absent from `MapView.cells`).
- **FR-005**: The uniform per-tile inner shadow MUST no longer be rendered; boundary-based bevels (FR-001) replace it. Sub-pass 1c MUST paint each land tile's face as a **fixed decorative gradient** in its biome zone's colour: the zone's configured midpoint at the tile's NW corner, `LAND_FACE_GRADIENT_DELTA = −5` HSL lightness points darker (hue and saturation unchanged) at its SE corner. The texture is a function of zone and zoom alone — every tile of a zone draws identical pixels — and encodes nothing about elevation; the bevels remain the sole relief cue and the texture MUST stay well below their tone shifts, so it reads as surface and never as a lit or shaded edge. It MUST be pre-rendered once per zone rather than created per cell per frame. The zone 2–3 contour hints (`024-FR-012`) MUST remain unchanged, as MUST the DOM overlay's transparency (`024-FR-020`). This resolves the conflict with 005's absorbed `024-FR-013` ("The inner shadow on land tiles MUST remain unchanged") and the per-tile gradient clause of `024-FR-011`, both rewritten by amendment A-01.
- **FR-006**: Relief MUST be distinguishable on all four biome zones, including the brightest (Peaks: `hue: 200`, `saturationPct: 15`, `lightnessMin: 80`, `lightnessMax: 95`). On each zone's **reference fixture** — own elevation at that zone's `elevationMax`, a visible land neighbour one lower on the W side (lit) and one lower on the E side (shaded), so both bevels are the weakest Δ1 step — **(a)** the lit and shaded bevels MUST measure ≥ 3:1 WCAG relative-luminance contrast against each other; **(b)** each bevel MUST differ from the face by ≥ **1.2:1**, measured against the face gradient's worst-case stop for that tone — a lit (N/W) bevel against the lightest stop (the zone colour at the NW corner), a shaded (S/E) bevel against the darkest stop (`LAND_FACE_GRADIENT_DELTA` at the SE corner). Both are floors at their stated values, one constant for every zone, never a per-zone threshold table, and both are on the rendered result rather than a particular colour or blend. Measurements MUST sample away from the sub-pass 1e contour diagonals (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.10)` every 6 px, zones 2–3), whose composite can move a sampled pixel.
- **FR-007**: Relief MUST be purely visual. Flow rates, the uphill stall threshold, `classifyPipeSlope`/`pipeIntensity`, the pipe slope colours, and the slope triangles stay exactly as specified by 005 FR-013 and absorbed `024-FR-014`. Relief MUST NOT alter pipe rendering, order preflight, or any wire message.
- **FR-008**: Relief MUST be produced by the console's Canvas 2D painter. A WebGL/WebGL2 or 3D renderer for this effect is explicitly out of scope; the per-tile DOM invariant is `021-FR-004`'s (asserted by AC-007), so it is not restated here.
- **FR-009**: Every colour relief paints MUST come from `@europa/design` tokens (012 FR-009; absorbed `021-FR-006`/`021-FR-011`): `packages/console/src` MUST NOT gain a hex or rgb literal for relief (guard G-04, the running `check:no-literals` scan). Any new token MUST be additive and MUST land in `packages/design/src/tokens.ts` and `DESIGN.md` § 1.1 in the same commit (012 FR-017/FR-018; a minor change per `DESIGN.md` § 6). Because the relief pairing is orientation-dependent, delta-stepped, and canvas-painted, it is documented as a note in the `DESIGN.md` N-3/N-4 family and MUST NOT become a § 3 measured row — § 3's own preamble defines every row as a measured pairing pinned by a computed-style check over a rendered CSS fixture, which cannot read canvas pixels or a delta; and G-07, the guard behind that claim, is deferred per 012's Scope Note, so no runner would read it anyway. A documented pairing that cannot be asserted mechanically MUST have its provenance and guard gap recorded in that note.
- **FR-010**: Relief MUST be a pure, deterministic function of visible view data only — own terrain and elevation, the neighbour's terrain and elevation, and whether the neighbour is present in the fog-filtered view. It MUST NOT read wall-clock time, use unseeded randomness, depend on paint order or accumulated state, or require a new serialized view field. Painting the same `MapView` twice MUST produce byte-identical pixels, and so MUST painting the same cells after inserting them into `MapView.cells` in a different order (`MapView.cells` is a `Map`, so iteration order is insertion order — an order-sensitive treatment would ship a board that depends on how the view was built). The 1000-tick golden suite (`packages/console/tests/integration/determinism.test.ts`, 005 SC-002) MUST stay green with its hash unchanged.
- **FR-011**: The player-facing description MUST ship in the same change set: (a) `docs/manual/src/pages/the-board.mdx` MUST explain relief and MUST correct its stale elevation sentence — "from dark green at the lowest ground to bright green at the peaks" describes no shipped biome zone (hues are 210/170/30/200); (b) the in-match help overlay's Symbol Legend (absorbed `018-FR-004`, `packages/console/src/ui/help-overlay.tsx`) MUST gain entries distinguishing absolute biome elevation (hue) from relative edge relief (luminance) and MUST NOT keep "dark = low, light = high" — also false under the shipped zones, where zone 1 (Fractured Ice, 26–40) is darker than zone 0 (Ice Plains, 38–52) while being higher. The manual edit is required because `the-board.mdx:26` is already false, not because 007 FR-012 triggers on this feature (it is not gameplay behaviour). The same change set corrects `DESIGN.md` and the `palette.ts` comment through amendments A-03 and A-05, every corrected contrast figure produced by a test that reads `TOKENS.color.biomeZones`, never a hand-copied literal.

### Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-001 (Performance)**: The paint budget stays green with relief enabled, as one merge gate of two parts — no separate statistic may be substituted for either. **(a)** `test:perf` Q-P01 (`packages/console/tests/integration/perf.test.ts`) keeps its min-of-round-medians **8 ms** budget on its 32×32 fixture, with only the documented `EUROPA_PERF_BUDGET_FACTOR` CI slack. **(b)** The suite gains a relief-enabled **48×48** fixture — the supported maximum, 2,304 cells, all land, with a **representative delta distribution** (the plan pins the elevation formula and records the measured distinct positive `|Δ|` and distinct-tone-colour counts) — and gates its **relief-attributable addition**, not its absolute frame time: the min-of-round-medians paint time with relief enabled **minus** the same figure with relief disabled, on the identical fixture, MUST NOT exceed the bound the plan pins and the user approves, and that bound MUST be pinned with a recorded margin over the worst observed run, such that a ~1 % margin is visibly unacceptable. The test MUST assert both the addition and the fixture's delta variety, so a fixture cannot silently regress to a degenerate distribution that flatters the number. **The fixture's absolute frame time is deliberately not gated**, because a 48×48 full-board paint's base cost is unrelated to this feature: the bare board costs ≈0.65 ms with relief off with the face texture on (≈0.7 ms under v1.9–v1.10's flat face; ≈3.6 ms before the gradient was first retired), and a decorated board (units, pipes, cities) costs ≈40–50 ms with relief off. The bound's measured basis is context, not a criterion: `plan.md` D7 owns the measured figures — the relief-attributable addition and its worst observed run — and this spec deliberately does not restate them, so the two records cannot drift. Relief MUST NOT be made cheaper by weakening the model's geometry or by adding steps beyond the three FR-003 pins.
- **NFR-002 (Simplicity and self-hosting)**: Zero new runtime dependencies; relief is computed in-process from the view the client already receives. No engine, protocol, or configuration surface changes (constitution Principles V and VII).

### Key Entities

None. Relief adds no data entity, no contract field, and no persisted state; it is a paint-time function of existing `MapView`/`CellRenderInfo` fields (`terrain`, `elevation`, cell presence) and `MapView.width`/`height`.

## Acceptance Criteria *(mandatory)*

Every criterion is binary and machine-checkable unless marked **reviewer check**.

**Done when** the readback suite passes on all four biome zones, `test:perf` is green with the relief-enabled 48×48 fixture, and `pnpm verify` is green.

### v1.9 (2026-10-20) — the settled flat-face / per-side bevel model

- **The face is flat and never lit.** Sub-pass 1c paints one colour per biome zone (the zone's configured midpoint); the per-tile gradient is retired. All three-dimensional reading comes from 1d's bevels. The zone hue channel and 1e's contour hints stay.

- **Geometry and caps.** Each bevel is the mitred trapezoid specified in FR-001; the taper marks a run end. At every grid vertex, ends are gathered from all four surrounding tiles: two or more ends fill each one's cut triangle in its own band colour, one end keeps its taper. The cap rule closes collinear seams and perpendicular turns without run lengths.

### v1.10 (2026-10-20) — calibrated against the real elevation range, tone by lightness

- **The v1.9 ladder assumed a small heightfield and saturated everywhere.** Real cell elevation spans 0–255 (`landBandIndex`'s four zones), so adjacent land tiles commonly differ by far more than 3: measured over five seeds at both shipped board sizes (32 and 48; 28,594 adjacent land pairs from `@europa/terrain`), `|Δ|` runs p25 = 2, p50 = 4, p75 = 7, p90 = 10, p99 = 15, max = 27. Only the Δ→step cut moved: **step 1 Δ 1–4, step 2 Δ 5–9, step 3 Δ ≥ 10**, which splits the measured positive drops 51 % / 40 % / 8 %. `RELIEF_STEP_THRESHOLDS = [4, 9]` in `relief.ts`; `plan.md` D5 owns the measurement.
